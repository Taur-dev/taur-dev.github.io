Questions, answered

What do I get for free? +

The whole homestead core: tasks and reminders, a shared calendar with .ics export, a full budget with reports, the garden with beds and harvest logs, local weather, a photo gallery, and notes. It's a complete app on its own — you never have to buy anything.

What are the modules, and what do they cost? +

Poultry, goats, rabbits, and equine are each a one-time $6.99 unlock that adds that animal's full record-keeping surface — the herd or flock, health, breeding, and species-specific production like eggs and milk. Buy only the ones you keep. There is no subscription anywhere in the app.

How is this different from Equine or Flock Companion? +

Equine and Flock Companion are single-animal apps for people who keep one kind of animal seriously. Homestead Companion is for a mixed place — a bit of everything, plus a garden and the money and chores that tie it all together — with one calendar and one budget across the lot.

Do I need to create an account? +

No. There's no sign-up, no email, no password. You open the app and your records live on your device from the first tap.

Where is my data stored? +

Entirely on your phone or tablet. There's no Taur Studios server holding your homestead's information, so there's nothing to be breached or sold. If you delete the app, your data goes with it — that's the point.

Does it work without internet? +

Yes. The app is fully usable offline. The optional online features are weather, which sends only an approximate location to Open-Meteo at request time, and voice-to-text, which uses your device's own speech recognition.
