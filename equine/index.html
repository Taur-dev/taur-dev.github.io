Your data stays on your device. Full stop.

Most apps in this space want an account, then quietly use your data to sell ads. Equine Companion does the opposite — it's built privacy-first, by one person, because your horses' records are yours.

There's no sign-up and no server holding your information. The app works fully offline. It only goes online for optional, feature-tied things: map tiles when you track a ride, a weather forecast if you turn it on (approximate location to Open-Meteo, which doesn't keep it), voice-to-text if you use it, and checking a Pro purchase.

  • No tracking or analytics SDKs. The app doesn't phone home.

Questions, answered

What is Equine Companion? +

Equine Companion is a private horse care and record-keeping app for iPhone, iPad and Android. It keeps health records, farrier, vaccine and deworming reminders, documents like Coggins papers, tack, feed and costs for every horse, and makes shareable PDF reports for your vet. Everything is stored on your device, and there's no account.

Is there a horse care app without a subscription? +

Yes, this is one. Equine Companion is free to download and use, with no time limit and unlimited horses. The optional Pro unlock is a one-time purchase ($9.99 in the US). There are no monthly or yearly fees.

What's free and what's in Pro? +

Free covers everyday horse keeping: unlimited horses, health records, reminders, the document vault, photos, tack room, budget, feed, PDF reports and encrypted backup. Pro adds GPS ride tracking, training and competition logs, a breeding module, and trainer mode for clients and lessons.

Can it remind me about the farrier, vaccines and deworming? +

Yes. Set recurring or one-off reminders for farrier visits, vaccinations, deworming and vet checks. They're local notifications, so they work without signal, and you can export them to your phone's calendar.

Can I keep Coggins papers and vet records in the app? +

Yes. The document vault holds Coggins tests, registration papers, bills of sale and any PDF, next to the horse they belong to. Health records cover vaccinations, deworming, vet visits, dental and lab results.

Can I share a horse's records with my vet or a new owner? +

Yes. Make a PDF horse report, choose what goes in it, and share it however you like. Nothing passes through a server on the way.

Can it track my rides? +

Yes, with Pro. GPS ride tracking records your route, distance and the weather for each ride, and the training log keeps sessions and goals for each horse.

Does it work for trainers and barns with lots of horses? +

Yes. Horse profiles are unlimited in the free version. Pro adds trainer mode for clients, students, lessons, scheduling, services and income, plus competition and breeding records.

Do I need to create an account? +

No. There's no sign-up, no email, no password. You open the app and your records live on your device from the first tap.

Where is my data stored, and can I move it to a new phone? +

Your records are stored only on your phone or tablet, not on a server. Use the encrypted backup to save everything, photos included, and restore it on your new device.

Does it work without internet? +

Yes. It only goes online for optional, feature-tied things: map tiles when you track a ride, a weather forecast if you turn weather on (your approximate location goes to Open-Meteo, which doesn't keep it), voice-to-text if you use it, and checking a Pro purchase with the app store.

Is it available on iPhone and Android? +

Yes. It's on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.

Who makes Equine Companion? +

I do. I'm Taur, and Taur Studios is my one-person studio. I grew up around horses and built this because no app got privacy and breadth right at the same time. I also make Flock, Goat and Homestead Companion. Questions and ideas go to taurstudios@gmail.com.
